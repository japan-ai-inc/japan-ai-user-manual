# JAPAN AI Studio User Manual

This manual explains how to use JAPAN AI Studio ("Studio") from the perspective of **people who use it, build with it, administer it, develop on it, and distribute from it**.

You do not need to read everything from the beginning. Choose the article in the cards below that is closest to what you want to do now.

If you want to build without writing code, tell **Studio Assistant** in a Project what you need and ask it to build within the Project (Agents, Workflows, Pages, and more). Studio Assistant requires the feature to be enabled for your organization and Editor or higher permission in the Project.

## About this manual

- The content is based on the production environment as of November 16, 2026, and on `@japan-ai-inc/studio-sdk` 0.11.0. Dated changes are listed in the [Release Notes](troubleshooting/release-notes.md).
- On-screen names are written in double quotation marks.
- Values such as limits, counts, and durations are the values as of the date above. Where a value depends on your contract plan or organization settings, the relevant article says so.
- If the display or behavior in your environment differs from this manual, check with your organization's administrator.
