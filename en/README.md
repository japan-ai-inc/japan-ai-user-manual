# JAPAN AI Studio User Manual

This manual explains how to use JAPAN AI Studio ("Studio") from the perspective of **people who use it, build with it, administer it, develop on it, and distribute from it**.

You do not need to read everything from the beginning. Choose the article in the cards below that is closest to what you want to do now.

## About this manual

- The content is based on the Studio implementation as confirmed on November 9, 2026, and on `@japan-ai-inc/studio-sdk` 0.11.0. The differences up to the November 10 production update (v1.22.20) were also reviewed, and the changes relevant to this manual are reflected.
- On-screen names are written in double quotation marks.
- Values such as limits, counts, and durations are the values at the time of confirmation. They may differ depending on your contract plan, your organization's settings, permissions, and availability.
- If the display or behavior in your environment differs from this manual, check with your organization's administrator.
