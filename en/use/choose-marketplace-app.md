# Choose an App on the Marketplace
Marketplace is your gateway to finding Studio Apps for your organization. In this article, we will explain how to find candidates, check their suitability for your business, required authority, external connections, and costs, and decide whether to introduce them.
**Being displayed on the list, being able to install it, and being able to use it for work are different things. ** Even if you find an app, installation permissions, connection settings, user addition, etc. may be required.
Confirm installation is handled by [Install App from Marketplace](install-marketplace-app.md), and initial settings after installation are handled by [Complete initial settings of App](complete-app-setup.md). This article does not require you to install an app or register credentials for verification.
## Before you start
Organize the following points.
- The target organization and the business you want to solve
- Main users and the number of people who are planning to use them
- Information to be handled and external services to which you plan to connect
- Person in charge of approving implementation, costs, and external connections
- Is it possible to do this with an app already installed in the organization?
If the app you want to use has already been announced, please refer to [Open the installed app and start using it](open-installed-app.md).
> The following explanation focuses on the standard screen on a PC web browser. The displayed menus, apps, buttons, and price information vary depending on your organization's settings, permissions, provision status, etc. Please check the conditions displayed on the screen and your organization's contract and operating rules.
## 1. Open Marketplace and search for suggestions
1. Check whether the currently selected organization is the organization you are considering implementing.
2. Open the app entrance such as "Studio Apps".
3. Select "Marketplace".
4. Enter the app name or related words in the search field to narrow down the list.
5. Check the name, short description, tags, provider, and installation status of the candidate.
6. Open the app details using actions such as "View details".
Standard search allows you to narrow down your search by app name, short description, or words contained in tags. If you are looking for a long sentence and cannot find it, try changing it to a shorter word. The full text of the detailed description and all synonyms may not be searched.
If Marketplace is not displayed, check with your administrator to see if it is open in another organization or if it is available in the target organization. You don't need to change any settings or permissions yourself to see it.
## 2. Check if it fits our business
Read the app overview, images, videos, updated information, etc. The type and details of posted information vary depending on the app.
| Things to check | Points to consider |
|---|---|
| Target work | Does the work we want to do match the purpose described by the app |
| Information to input | Can you prepare the necessary materials and data? Is the information permitted to be used? |
| Results | Can you get the output and work results you want? What is the range that people check |
| Source | Who provides the app? Can I check the contact information for installation and operation? |
| User | Who will use it? Is there someone in charge of adding and managing users after implementation? |
| Update/Operation | Can I check updated information and guidance? Is there someone in charge of checking during operation? |
Even if the information is marked as "official" or "certified," we will separately check its suitability for your business and whether it is okay to handle your organization's information. Display alone does not guarantee the accuracy of the output or internal approval within the organization.
If there are any points that you do not understand from the explanation, do not assume that they are supported, but leave them as things to check before implementation.
## 3. Check permissions and connection conditions
### Permissions required by App
Check the details such as "Information" and "Required permissions". If the authority name is technical and cannot be determined, convey the information on the screen to the person in charge and check what data can be done.
In particular, please review whether the following points are consistent with your business permission.
- Do you only want to read the information, or do you also want to register, change, delete, etc.
- Does it involve sending externally or updating external services?
- Which user/connected account's authority should be used for processing?
- Does it match the range of data that can be shown to users?
These are things to check when deciding to introduce the product. Not everything is explained on the details screen. If you have any questions, please check with the person in charge of the app.
**"Required permissions" does not indicate that you have the installation permissions. ** Please check separately the permission to install the app, the permission to access the app after installation, and the permission to operate external services.
### Connecting to external services
Check "Setup", "Credentials required after installation", etc., and confirm whether the displayed service can be used in your organization.
| Things to check | Things to decide before implementing |
|---|---|
| Required services | Do you have a usage contract or account |
| Account to connect | Whose account to use and what data to access |
| Connection method | Connection for each individual or connection shared by Project/App |
| Person in charge of settings | Who performs connection, authorization, and registration of secret information |
| Operations staff | Who will respond to disconnections, changes in personnel, and updates to authentication information |
Even if "No additional authentication information is required" is displayed, it does not mean that all initial settings, such as adding users and preparing business data, are not necessary.
There is no need to paste passwords, API keys, or access tokens into the selection consultation. Tell them the name of the service you need and whether there is someone who can set it up.
## 4. Check the price and number of users
If a fee is displayed, check not only the amount but also the unit, number of people, current cost, and continuing cost.
On the standard screen, "free" or "monthly seat system" may be displayed, or the unit price may be displayed as "credits/seats/month." Prices displayed are not necessarily in yen. Please check without changing the units on the screen.
| Check items | Points to see |
|---|---|
| App price classification | Is it free, or is there a charge depending on the number of people and seats |
| Unit price and unit | For what, in what period, and how many credits |
| Planned number of users | Who will use it, including the administrator? What are the conditions when adding users? |
| Cost of introduction | How much will be billed/credit consumed this time |
| Continuation costs | How the monthly amount and next renewal date are displayed |
| Costs to be confirmed separately | Does it include consumption during AI execution, contracts for external services, etc. |
Don't just look at the unit price details to determine the cost for your entire organization. If a fee is shown on the installation confirmation screen, please check the target number, current cost, and renewal conditions again before finalizing the installation. It may also be displayed that the amount used by the administrator is included.
**"Free" does not mean that there are no costs for using it for business purposes. ** Check separately the app charges and the charges for runtime consumption and external services. Also, the fact that the price is not displayed does not qualify it as free.
If you are unsure of the fees and renewal suspension conditions when increasing or decreasing the number of people or terminating use, please check with the person in charge before implementing the service. In this article, we do not uniformly specify operations for changing the number of people, canceling a contract, or refund conditions.
## 5. Check the displayed installation method
Depending on the app and current status, the display will be as follows.
| Main display | What to do next |
|---|---|
| Installation | After completing the selection and approval, proceed to the installation procedure. Reconfirm the details and costs on the confirmation screen |
| Contact JAPAN AI | Without assuming that you will be implementing it yourself, tell us the purpose, organization, expected number of users, and confirmation items |
| Information regarding installation privileges | Request a person in charge, such as an organization administrator, who can confirm privileges |
| Installed/Open | Check if you can use an existing app instead of installing a new one |
| Participation/Ask administrator | Check participation/access to installed apps. If you select "Participate", you may be required to join the App Project before opening it, so you cannot proceed with the join operation if you only intend to view it. |
| Installing | Check the current processing status. Do not repeat new installation operations |
| Installation failure/retry | Apart from selection, check the person in charge of execution and the current status |
| Information that it is currently unavailable | Inform the administrator or support of the target app and the displayed content |
Permissions are not the only reason why "Install" is not displayed. Apps may be introduced through inquiries, or may depend on the organization's provision/purchase conditions. Don't assume that an administrator can deploy every app directly.
## 6. Summary of selection results
Summarize the following items so that the person in charge of implementation can make a decision. Please leave unconfirmed items as "unconfirmed" to distinguish them from approved items.
> - **Apps to be considered for introduction**: App name/details screen URL
> - **Deployed to**: Organization name
> - **Application**: Target business and expected results
> - **Users**: Number of expected users/person in charge of management
> - **Required authority**: Confirmed/Unconfirmed matters
> - **External connection**: Required services, connection method, and setting person
> - **Cost**: Screen price classification, unit price, unit, update conditions
> - **Installation method**: Direct installation/Contact JAPAN AI/Confirmation required
> - **Approval status**: Approver, scope of approval, remaining confirmation items
Sharing the URL of the details screen does not give you permission to view or install it. Also check if the person you're sharing with can see information from the same organization.
Once you have confirmed the above, proceed to [Install App from Marketplace](install-marketplace-app.md). For apps that require inquiries, please follow the instructions for contacting them.
## When things don't go well
| Situation | Things to check first |
|---|---|
| Marketplace not found | Selected organization, provision status in the organization, available entrances |
| No matching apps | Shorten your search term, change it to a different term, or delete your search term |
| Displayed when there are no published apps | There are no candidates in the current list. Distinguish from loading error |
| Loading fails | Follow the on-screen instructions and reconfirm. Don't treat failure as "the app doesn't exist" |
| I don't understand the meaning of permissions or connections. | Tell the person in charge about the displayed content and purpose. Don't guess what's allowed |
| I don't know the price | Don't assume it's free, check with the app's representative or support |
| Unable to select installation | Installation method, authority, purchase/provision conditions, current installation status |
When making an inquiry, please tell us the organization name, app name, date and time of confirmation, display content, and the task you would like to perform. Don't unnecessarily include sensitive data or credentials.

- [ ] Confirmed the implementation method, person in charge, and necessary approvals.
